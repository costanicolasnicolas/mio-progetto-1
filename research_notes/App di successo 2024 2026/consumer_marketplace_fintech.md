# Consumer apps, marketplaces, fintech and prediction/commerce platforms that exploded in 2024-2026 (non-AI)

Research date: 2026-11-26. All figures come from press reports of company announcements unless marked otherwise. Items marked [S] are third-party estimates or single-source reports. Items marked [BK] are background knowledge from before this run and were NOT checked here; verify them before publishing.

Companies covered (9): Kalshi, Polymarket, Whatnot, Pump.fun, Revolut, Ramp, Substack, Hamster Kombat/Notcoin (Telegram mini-apps), RedNote/Xiaohongshu (US surge). Not covered (gaps): Temu/Shein, Bluesky/Threads, Strava.

## 1. What each company does, when it exploded, founders

### Takeaway
Prediction markets (Kalshi, Polymarket) and live commerce (Whatnot) are the biggest new fortunes of 2024-2026. Their valuations rose about 4-20x in under 18 months. Fintech (Revolut, Ramp) compounded from an already large base. Crypto consumer apps (Pump.fun, Hamster Kombat) went viral but faded fast.

## 3. What worked: growth mechanisms and revenue models

### Takeaway
There were four growth engines. (a) Regulatory openings plus news events (prediction markets). (b) Live, social, community commerce (Whatnot). (c) Crypto token incentives (Pump.fun, Telegram tap-to-earn). (d) Product breadth, cross-selling and interest income (Revolut, Ramp).

### Cited Findings
- **Prediction markets**: Kalshi raised to expand overseas and into institutional sales (Series D/F/G). The Series G funds new products, institutional sales, Latin America and Europe. The NYT expects it to be the last round before an IPO — [Bloomberg Law](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/private-equity/kalshi-raises-funds-at-5-billion-valuation-to-expand-overseas); [Tech Funding News](https://techfundingnews.com/kalshi-nearly-doubles-to-40b-in-five-months-with-1b-from-sequoia-and-wellington/)
- Polymarket's US return came after it acquired a CFTC-licensed exchange and clearinghouse (QCEX, about $112M) that received a CFTC no-action letter. The US app rolled out to waitlisted users in early Dec 2025 — [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/polymarket-set-reopen-us-tomorrow-115516899.html); [Cointelegraph](https://cointelegraph.com/news/polymarket-us-relaunch-waitlisted-users)
- ICE's investment gave Polymarket distribution of its event data to institutional clients and legitimacy [BK on the data-distribution detail] — [Orrick](https://www.orrick.com/fr-FR/News/2025/10/Intercontinental-Exchange-announced-it-will-make-a-strategic-investment-in-Polymarket)
- Revenue model: transaction fees on event contracts [BK on fee detail]
- **Whatnot**: live auctions plus community. It takes a seller commission [BK on rate] and is expanding internationally and into seller tools and trust and safety (Series G) — [PYMNTS](https://pymnts.com/news/investment-tracker/2026/live-commerce-platform-whatnot-raises-545-million-to-speed-ai-expansion)
- **Pump.fun**: anyone can launch a token in seconds via a bonding curve, and the platform takes fees on trades. Commentary suggested PUMP holders would get a share of revenue (reported 25%, unconfirmed). The ICO reopened the "ICO era" — [CryptoSlate](https://cryptoslate.com/memecoin-platform-pumpfun-concludes-one-of-the-fastest-icos-raising-600m-in-12-minutes/); [Blockworks](https://blockworks.co/news/pump-fun-500m-ico-twitch)
- Pump.fun aims to compete with Twitch via creator livestreams — [Blockworks](https://blockworks.co/news/pump-fun-500m-ico-twitch)
- **Telegram tap-to-earn**: viral referral loops inside Telegram's existing user base, plus the promise of a token airdrop. 60% of HMSTR went to players. The airdrop was on Sept 26 2024 on TON, delayed from July — [CoinMarketCap Academy](https://coinmarketcap.com/academy/article/hamster-kombat-reveals-details-of-massive-crypto-airdrop); [KuCoin](https://www.kucoin.com/learn/crypto/what-is-hamster-kombat-telegram-crypto-game)
- **Revolut**: about 25% of 2024 revenue came from interest on customer deposits. It also earns from cards, trading, subscriptions and its business accounts — [Banking Dive](https://bankingdive.com/news/revolut-valued-75-billion/806502)
- **Ramp**: interchange on corporate cards plus software. Revenue and customers doubled in the year to Nov 2025. It now has an "AI" narrative but is a fintech core — [FF News](https://ffnews.com/newsarticle/ramp-reaches-32-billion-valuation-doubling-revenue-and-customers-in-past-year/); [American Banker](https://www.americanbanker.com/news/ramp-raises-750-million-plans-ai-spending-software)
- **Substack**: takes 10% of paid subscriptions [BK]. It grew through creator migration and a social/app layer — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/07/17/substack-raises-100m-from-chernin-group-andreessen-horowitz-skims-ceo-and-more/)
- **RedNote**: an exogenous shock (the TikTok ban law upheld by the Supreme Court) plus "ironic protest" sentiment. DFRLab notes steady growth for six months before the ban — [DFRLab](https://dfrlab.org/2025/02/24/tiktok-xiaohongshu-rednote-us-china/)

### Inferences
- Prediction markets monetize news cycles directly (elections, then sports, which now drive most volume). Each viral event acts as free marketing.
- Token incentives buy explosive user acquisition, but retention collapses after the airdrop (see the Hamster Kombat "dust" complaints).

### Gaps
- Exact take rates for Whatnot, Kalshi and Polymarket were not verified in this run.

## 4. Incumbents and site types beaten or disrupted

### Takeaway
Prediction markets attack sportsbooks, opinion polls and parts of exchange-traded derivatives. Whatnot attacks eBay, QVC/HSN and card shops. Revolut and Ramp take share from banks and from Amex/expense software. RedNote and Bluesky-type apps were "exit valves" from TikTok and X rather than lasting winners.

### Cited Findings
- Kalshi's $40B valuation "tests Wall Street's turf", positioning it against exchanges — [Finimize](https://finimize.com/content/kalshis-40-billion-valuation-tests-wall-streets-turf)
- An exchange incumbent (ICE/NYSE) chose to invest in Polymarket rather than fight it — [Fortune](https://fortune.com/crypto/2025/10/07/polymarket-2-billion-intercontinental-exchange-new-york-stock-exchange-9-billion/)
- State regulators protecting licensed sportsbooks and casinos are fighting Kalshi and Polymarket over sports contracts: more than 20 lawsuits — [NPR](https://npr.org/2026/04/02/nx-s1-5771635/trump-cftc-kalshi-polymarket-lawsuits)
- Revolut's valuation is reported to exceed Barclays' — [Finance Magnates](https://www.financemagnates.com/trending/revolut-valuation-tops-barclays-a-new-era-for-challenger-banks/)
- Pump.fun is positioned against Twitch in livestreaming — [Blockworks](https://blockworks.co/news/pump-fun-500m-ico-twitch)
- RedNote was a substitute for TikTok during the ban scare — [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2025/01/15/nx-s1-5260742/tiktok-ban-china-rednote-xiaohongshu-app)

### Inferences
- Whatnot vs eBay/QVC: live, entertainment-driven auctions in collectibles (cards, Pokemon, sneakers) take share from static listings and TV shopping. No source in this run quantified eBay's lost share, so this is an inference.
- Polling: prediction markets are now quoted in the media next to polls, but no source in this run measured displacement.

### Gaps
- No quantitative data was found on incumbents' losses (eBay GMV impact, sportsbook handle diverted, bank deposit outflows).
- Bluesky/Threads vs X was not researched in this run.

## 5. Regulatory risks and controversies

### Takeaway
Prediction markets face the biggest legal fight: federal (CFTC) preemption against state gambling law, now including criminal charges in Arizona and a ban in Minnesota. Pump.fun faces securities and RICO class actions. Telegram airdrops disappointed users. RedNote raised China-data concerns.

### Cited Findings
- **Polymarket**: a 2022 CFTC settlement ($1.4M, US users barred). The FBI seized Coplan's devices in a Nov 2024 raid on his NYC apartment. The DOJ and CFTC closed their probes without charges (reported by Bloomberg July 15 2025), clearing the path for its US return — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-07-15/polymarket-probe-ended-by-doj-in-win-for-crypto-bets-under-trump); [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/news/regulation/2025-07-15-us-justice-department-and-cftc-close-probes-into-polymarket-bloomberg-362703)
- The CFTC is again examining whether Polymarket excluded US users as the 2022 settlement required (2026 reporting) — [IBTimes UK](https://www.ibtimes.co.uk/cftc-investigates-kalshi-polymarket-prediction-markets-1832581)
- Politico-type concerns about political ties: the $21B round was led by Trump Jr.-linked 1789 Capital. The Next Web notes the valuation assumes the CFTC prevails and sports bans fail — [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/polymarket-1789-capital-1bn-round-21bn)
- Norway opened a probe into abnormal Polymarket bets on a Nobel Prize winner (an insider-trading concern) [S, headline only] — [CryptoSlate](https://cryptoslate.com/doj-and-cftc-end-probes-into-polymarket-clearing-path-for-potential-registration/)
- **Kalshi and the states**: in April 2026 the DOJ and CFTC sued Illinois, Arizona and Connecticut to preempt state regulation. The CFTC later sued Arkansas (Nov 13 2026), and Kentucky became the ninth state sued — [PBS](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/economy/federal-government-sues-three-states-for-trying-to-regulate-prediction-markets); [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/04/04/feds-sue-3-states-for-trying-to-bring-kalshi-and-polymarket-under-more-control/)
- Arizona filed the first state criminal charges against Kalshi, and Minnesota enacted the first outright ban on prediction markets (challenged by the CFTC, Kalshi and Polymarket). Politico (Nov 10 2026) reported that Kalshi received a private warning about possible charges [S]. There are more than 20 lawsuits over sports event contracts. Kalshi is also in a legal dispute with Utah — [NPR](https://npr.org/2026/04/02/nx-s1-5771635/trump-cftc-kalshi-polymarket-lawsuits); [Newsweek](https://www.newsweek.com/map-shows-us-states-battling-trump-administration-over-prediction-markets-12178936); [Infor Capital](https://inforcapital.com/news/kalshi-raises-1b-series-g-at-40b-valuation-amid-legal-dispute-with-utah/)

# Consumer and creative AI apps born or exploded 2024-2026 that made their founders rich

Method note: every finding below comes from WebSearch result snippets (searched 2026-11-26). Full-page WebFetch was blocked by the repository's domain-allowlist hook (techcrunch.com, businesswire.com, elevenlabs.io, thenextweb.com, fastcompany.com are not in the catalogue), so the article text could not be checked in full. Legend: [CO] = figure stated by the company or its CEO; [IND] = independent reporting (Reuters, Bloomberg, WSJ, FT, The Information, TechCrunch reporting); [EST] = third-party estimate (Sacra, Upstarts, etc.); [LIT] = figure from a court filing by an opposing party.

## 1. What each product does, launch/explosion year, founders and background

### Takeaway
Ten companies are covered. Eight are US/EU/Asia startups founded 2020-2024 that exploded in 2024-2026: Perplexity, ElevenLabs, Suno, Gamma, Manus, Genspark, Granola and Higgsfield. Character.AI is included for its Aug-2024 Google deal, and Midjourney as a profitable incumbent. Most founders are ex-Big Tech or AI-lab engineers, and many had a prior exit.

### Cited Findings
- **Perplexity** (AI answer engine with citations; also the Comet browser). Founded 2022 by Aravind Srinivas (CEO), Denis Yarats (CTO), Johnny Ho and Andy Konwinski — [Contrary Research](https://research.contrary.com/company/perplexity)
- **ElevenLabs** (AI voice: text-to-speech, cloning, dubbing, voice agents). Co-founded 2022 by Piotr Dąbkowski (ex-Google ML engineer) and Mati Staniszewski (ex-Palantir deployment strategist, CEO) — [Wikiwand/ElevenLabs](https://wikiwand.com/en/articles/ElevenLabs); [TIME100 AI 2024: Dabkowski](https://www.aol.com/time100-ai-2024-piotr-dabkowski-110441435.html)
- **Suno** (text-to-song music generator). Its four co-founders all came from Kensho, the financial AI company acquired by S&P Global: Mikey Shulman (Kensho head of ML, CEO), Georg Kucsko, Martin Camacho and Keenan Freyberg (COO, left Jan 2025). Founded in Cambridge, MA; sources disagree on 2022 vs 2023. Came out of stealth in Dec 2023 — [Contrary Research](https://research.contrary.com/reports/suno); [Axios 2023](https://www.axios.com/2023/12/20/suno-gen-ai-music-microsoft)
- **Gamma** (AI presentations, documents and websites; positioned as a PowerPoint replacement). Founded late 2020 by Grant Lee, Jon Noronha and James Fox, all from Optimizely. It raised $7M in Oct 2021, so it predates ChatGPT and exploded after it added generative AI — [a16z](https://a16z.com/announcement/investing-in-gamma/); [TechCrunch 2021](https://techcrunch.com/2021/10/28/gamma-brings-in-7m-to-bring-the-slide-deck-into-the-21st-century)
- **Manus** (general-purpose autonomous AI agent that executes tasks). Built by Butterfly Effect, founded in Beijing in 2022 and later moved to Singapore. Leader Xiao Hong previously launched the Monica.ai assistant; Yichao "Peak" Ji is co-founder and chief scientist. Launched 5-6 March 2025 — [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/2025/12/30/meta_acquires_manus/); [China Daily](https://www.chinadaily.com.cn/a/202503/06/WS67c9ba2fa310c240449d9276.html); [Laptop Mag](https://www.laptopmag.com/ai/what-is-manus-ai)
- **Genspark** (MainFunc Inc.; first an AI search engine with "Sparkpages" in June 2024, then an agentic "AI Workspace" from April 2025). Founded in the US in 2023 by Eric Jing (ex-Baidu executive, CEO of Xiaodu, earlier on Microsoft Bing) and Kay Zhu (ex-Google search ranking, ex-CTO of Xiaodu), plus Wen Sang (MIT PhD, prior exit with Smarking). Seed round of $60M led by Lanchi Ventures at a $260M valuation — [TechCrunch Jun 2024](https://techcrunch.com/2024/06/18/genspark-is-the-latest-attempt-at-an-ai-powered-search-engine); [TMTPost](https://en.tmtpost.com/post/7135172)
- **Granola** (AI meeting notepad without a bot, now an "enterprise context" app). Founders Chris Pedregal (CEO, previously sold Socratic to Google) and designer Sam Stephenson (ex-Ideaflow). Public debut May 2024 — [TechCrunch May 2024](https://techcrunch.com/2024/05/22/granola-debuts-an-ai-notepad-for-meetings); [TechCrunch Oct 2024](https://techcrunch.com/2024/10/23/vcs-love-using-the-ai-meeting-notepad-granola-so-they-gave-it-20m)
- **Higgsfield** (AI video and image generation and orchestration platform for creators and marketers). Founded and launched April 2024 by Alex Mashrabov, ex-head of generative AI at Snap, whose earlier startup AI Factory was sold to Snap for $166M. Sources give the sale as 2020 (TechCrunch) or 2019 (Higgsfield) — [TechCrunch Apr 2024](https://techcrunch.com/2024/04/03/former-snap-ai-chief-launches-higgsfield-to-take-on-openais-sora-video-generator); [Higgsfield team page](https://blog.higgsfield.ai/team)
- **Character.AI** (role-play chatbots and personas). Founders Noam Shazeer and Daniel De Freitas, both ex-Google. In Aug 2024 they returned to Google under a licensing deal — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2024-08-02/character-ai-co-founders-hired-by-google-in-licensing-deal)
- **Midjourney** (image generator). Founded 2021 by David Holz; self-funded subscription business — [Nieman Lab](https://www.niemanlab.org/2025/06/piracy-is-piracy-disney-and-universal-sue-ai-image-generator-midjourney-in-a-hollywood-first/)

### Inferences
- Common pattern: senior builders from Big Tech or AI (Google, Snap, Baidu, Palantir, Optimizely, Kensho). Several are repeat founders with a prior exit (Pedregal, Mashrabov, Sang, Xiao Hong/Monica).
- Gamma and Perplexity predate the boom, but their revenue "explosion" falls in 2024-2026.

### Gaps

## 3. What worked: growth mechanism and revenue model

### Takeaway
Three engines dominate. First, outputs that can be shared (songs, videos, decks, Sparkpages) and that advertise the product. Second, freemium with credits and subscriptions (consumer-to-prosumer). Third, artificial scarcity or word of mouth among high-status users (Manus invite codes, Granola among VCs and founders). Many of these companies are lean and some are profitable (Gamma, Midjourney).

### Cited Findings
- Gamma: organic, product-led growth; the CEO describes building a $100M business "differently" with a small team. Profitable, so the round was optional — [Gamma insights](https://gamma.app/insights/how-we-built-a-usd100m-business-differently); [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/gamma-grant-lee-profitable-ai-startup)
- Suno: 90% of daily users create music instead of only listening (Shulman) — a "creator-first" model; subscriptions (2M paid) — [Sequoia podcast](https://sequoiacap.com/podcast/sunos-mikey-shulman-everyone-can-make-music-now); [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/07/17/suno-raised-400-million-lawsuits-sony-universal-menlo-ventures-user-behavior)
- Manus: viral demo video plus invite-only access (1% admitted), which fed a resale market and a 2M waitlist; positioned as China's "next DeepSeek moment" — [Bloomberg Opinion](https://www.bloomberg.com/opinion/articles/2025-03-13/manus-ai-pushes-the-deepseek-moment-further); [China Daily](https://www.chinadaily.com.cn/a/202503/06/WS67c9ba2fa310c240449d9276.html)
- Granola: mostly organic growth (Betaworks); 10% weekly growth; more than half of users in leadership roles (57%); a 6-month beta with 150 users that cut features — [Betaworks](https://www.betaworks.com/writing/granola-has-officially-become-a-unicorn); [TechCrunch Oct 2024](https://techcrunch.com/2024/10/23/vcs-love-using-the-ai-meeting-notepad-granola-so-they-gave-it-20m)
- Higgsfield: aggregator and orchestrator of third-party video models (sells credits for multiple models) aimed at social creators and marketers; Sacra calls it a "Pinterest for AI video" — [Sacra interview](https://sacra.com/research/alex-mashrabov-higgsfield-ai-video-production/); [Sacra](https://sacra.com/research/100m-yr-pinterest-for-ai-video/)
- Perplexity: subscriptions (Pro/Max), publisher revenue sharing ($42.5M pool, 80% of Comet Plus revenue to publishers), Comet browser, query growth of 20% month on month — [Keywords Everywhere](https://keywordseverywhere.com/news/perplexity-updates/); [TechCrunch Jun 2025](https://techcrunch.com/2025/06/05/perplexity-received-780-million-queries-last-month-ceo-says)
- Genspark: pivot from search to an agentic "AI Workspace" (Apr 2025); now hiring a CRO, so an enterprise push — [BusinessWire Jun 2026](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260617758937/en/)
- Midjourney: subscription only, no VC, a very small team — [Nieman Lab](https://www.niemanlab.org/2025/06/piracy-is-piracy-disney-and-universal-sue-ai-image-generator-midjourney-in-a-hollywood-first/)

### Inferences
- The typical trajectory is consumer virality, then prosumer subscriptions, then enterprise (Granola Team Spaces, Genspark CRO, ElevenLabs agents, Gamma teams). Enterprise is the move that defends against commoditization.
- Wrapper or aggregator models (Higgsfield, Genspark, Manus built on third-party models) can grow very fast but have thinner margins and less defensibility.

- Gross margins: no verified figures.

## 5. Team size at key milestones and controversies

### Takeaway
The teams are extremely lean: Gamma ~30 people at $50M ARR and ~50 at $100M ARR, and Midjourney ~11 FTE (dated figure). Higgsfield reports ~300 (secondary source). Perplexity has grown to over 1,000 (estimates vary). Controversies centre on copyright (Suno, Midjourney, Perplexity), user safety (Character.AI), voice consent (ElevenLabs), China ties (Manus) and agent scraping (Perplexity Comet vs Amazon).

### Inferences
- Copyright litigation has not stopped fundraising. Suno doubled its valuation while being sued, so investors appear to price in settlements or licensing (the Warner precedent).
